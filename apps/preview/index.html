VIDEO.JS V10 SKINS FROM IBOY

Seven skins.
Seven worlds.

Every skin rebuilds the paused frame in its own material and draws the seek bar from the sound you have heard. Pause the film to see it. Pick a skin below.

FOOTAGE

SIZES

Every skin, every width

The controls follow the width of the player itself, not the window. Narrow players drop the title and the cast and picture-in-picture buttons, hand the centre cluster over to the panel, and keep every button thumb sized. These two follow the skin you pick above.

ACCESS

Made for everyone

  • Readable over any footage

    Controls sit on a solid panel in every skin, never on the picture.

  • Keyboard first

    Every control is reachable in visual order, with a thick focus ring that always shows.

  • Thumb sized

    Buttons are 44 px on touch screens and never smaller than 40 px elsewhere.

  • Calm on request

    With reduced motion on, the frame effects appear finished without a transition, bouncing and hopping stop, and the seek bar redraws only a few times a second.

  • Forced colours and contrast

    High contrast and forced colours modes swap every material for solid system colours.

  • Never blocks playback

    The seek bar reads a copy of the audio and never routes it. If a browser or file cannot provide one, an even bar is drawn instead.

KEYBOARD

  • Space or K play and pause
  • J L or ← → seek
  • ↑ ↓ volume
  • M mute
  • F fullscreen
  • C captions
  • I picture in picture
  • 0 to 9 jump to a point

USE THEM

Open source, Apache 2.0

One small engine draws the frame and the seek bar. Each skin is a stylesheet of tokens plus a short renderer, so a new skin is a few hundred lines. Switch skins with data-skin on the player container.

data-skin
pinart, riso, cel, ledger, origami, tui or cortex
--media-accent-color
accent colour of the skin in use
--media-border-radius
player corners
--media-font-family
interface font
